### Introduction:

We have introduced a new feature in our [Multivendor Marketplace App](https://marketplace-doc.webkul.com/classic/Introduction/). This allows you to send custom notifications to your sellers.

Check the below video of Seller Notification:

### Configuration

Let's dive into the process to configure this feature in your multivendor app for Shopify.

To enable this, please visit-  
Multivendor Admin Panel > Sellers > Sellers Notification

![Seller-Notification-Admin](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Seller-Notification-Admin-1200x636.png)

Clicking on the Seller Notification you would be redirected to the page where you can add as well as check notifications

### Add Notification

You can click on "ADD NOTIFICATION" in order to create a new notification for the sellers

![add-notification](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/add-notification-1200x597.png)

To add a new notification you need to enter the title as well as the description of the notification. Now click on submit.

**NOTE-** The title limit is 100 characters and the limit for a notification message is 5000 characters.

![seller-noti](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/seller-noti-1200x587.png)

Now, click on submit and the same will be visible to the sellers on their respective seller panels accordingly.

### Seller Notifications

The latest notification added by the admin will appear on the seller dashboard as a pop-up when sellers will login to their seller dashboard.

![notification-pop](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/notification-pop-1200x642.png)

### Merchant Notifications

By clicking on the bell icon, the sellers will be able to view the top 5 recent notifications here added by the admin also they can click on view all to check all the previous notifications

![notification-bell](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/notification-bell.png)

Seller can also view their notification by logging in their panel than going to merchant notification as shown below:

![Merchant-notification-1](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Merchant-notification-1-1200x597.png)

### Admin Seller Notifications

Now, the admin can also check their created notification in the notification panel. Also, the admin can view & delete it by clicking on three dots.
