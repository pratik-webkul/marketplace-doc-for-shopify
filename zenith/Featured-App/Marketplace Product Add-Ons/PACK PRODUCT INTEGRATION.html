The Pack & Combo Product app is now integrated with the Multi-vendor Marketplace App for Shopify.

Enabling this feature, you can allow sellers to create pack and combo products from their seller panel. You as an admin can also add pack & combo products from your admin panel in the app.

What are Pack & Combo Products?

Pack products:- Pack products are basically a bundle product having two or more items added in just one package for sale.
There will be only one seller's products in a pack!

You can create the Package by adding two or more than two types of products in the pack.

Thus, the customers can purchase the whole items in just one purchase, accordingly price & quantity will be updated in the app.

Combo products:- Combo products are the combination of various products.
You may have different seller's products in a combo.

While creating a Combo, you can add various products to it and customers will purchase the combo product

by selecting proper variant, size, shipment separately for each product available in that Combo.

NOTE:- A pack will have multiple products from only one seller while in a combo, you can have different seller's products.

So, the Multi-vendor Pack Product Integration feature app let you bring in various products together to create packs & combos and have the best upselling method for your marketplace.

NOTE:- This integration is FREE, but to use this feature app, firstly you need to install the Pack & Combo Product App on your Shopify store.

Let's see how to configure this in the app.

Installation & Workflow

To enable the feature app, visit the featured apps section of the Multivendor Marketplace for Shopify.

And activate the "Pack Product Integration" feature app.
NOTE:- Make sure that you have enabled the Pack & Combo product App on your Shopify store.

After that, accept the charges and you are ready to use the app.

Admin End Configuration

As soon as you enable the feature app, two new sub-menus get added to the Product menu in the app i.e. Pack Product and Combo Product.

How To Add Pack Products?

Visit the Multi-vendor admin panel>>Products>>Click Pack Product.

Now, Click the Create Pack button on the top right corner of the page.

Clicking the Create Pack button, a form appears to enter the details for your new bundle product. Following are the details you need to enter:-

  • Name of your new Pack/Bundle Product.
  • Email of the seller to whom you wish to assign the pack/bundle product. Note:- That particular seller will be responsible for fulfillment.
  • Pack product price and quantity.
    Pack product Price:- Price of your bundle product.
    Actual product price:- Addition of all the product (added to the bundle) prices.
  • Add bundle product description, tags, collection & Images.

This is how you can add pack products and let customers purchase products in just one package.

Let's See How to Create a Combo Product.

How To Add Combo Products?

To add combo products to the app, Visit the Multi-vendor admin panel>>Go to Products>>Click Combo Products.

Now, enter the details of your Combo product including Combo product name, product description, tags.

Save The Changes.

The same way, sellers can add the Pack & Combo products from their seller panels.

Seller Panel

The sellers will also get the new sub-menus added to the Products menu of the seller panel i.e. Pack Product & Combo products.

Seller can add pack products and get the list of all the pack products added. Seller can anytime edit the details of the pack product as well as disable and delete them.

Similarly, the sellers can add, edit, delete the combo products.

This is how both admin and sellers can add pack & combo products using the app.

Let's check the visibility of the Pack & Combo products on the store frontend for the customers.

Configurating Front End

In order to show the pack & combo product details, you as an admin need to make sure that you have added the codes to Shopify theme template.

The code instructions are provided as an Important Note in the Home Page of the Pack & Combo Product App.

After you add the codes, this is how the products appear on your store front.
