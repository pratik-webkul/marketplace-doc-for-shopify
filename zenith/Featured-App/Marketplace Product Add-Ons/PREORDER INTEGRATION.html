PreOrder is a great approach to determine the product in high demand. You can allow pre-order on your marketplace products and earn even before the product release.

The Multi-vendor Marketplace app for Shopify is now integrated with the Pre-order Controller App.

Enabling the PreOrder feature app, you can auto-enable pre-order for the seller's products soon after the items get out of stock.

Thus, customers can preorder products from your marketplace by paying the preorder amount.

You can set the default preorder quantity for products.

The sellers can further edit the quantity from the seller panel. Also, disable the preorder on products manually while editing the products.

Note:- This integration works only in the case of Multi-product Checkout & Automatic PreOrder. Also, the Partial Payment won't work in case of the seller's preorder products.

Features

  • With the Multi-vendor Marketplace PreOrder Integration, you can enable the "Automatic PreOrder" option for the seller's products.
  • You can set a default pre-order quantity for products.
  • Anytime, you can edit the default pre-order quantity that will be a global setting applicable for all the seller's products on your marketplace.
  • The sellers can too edit the pre-order product quantity while editing the products from the seller panel.
  • Moreover, the sellers can disable pre-order on any specific products manually.
  • The Pre-Order feature app is also compatible with the Slot Pricing Feature App.
  • This integration is compatible with the Split Cart Feature app of the Multi-vendor app.

To make this feature work, you need to install the Pre-Order Controller app to your Shopify store. Once done, you can proceed to configure the settings.

Admin Configuration

AUTOMATIC PREORDER

In order to enable pre-order for the seller's products, firstly make sure you have selected the "Automatic PreOrder" from the Pre-order Configuration menu of the PreOrder Controller app.

For this, Go to the PreOrder Controller App.

Visit the Configurations>>PreOrder Configuration.

Here, select the PreOrder Type as "Automatic PreOrder" from the drop-down menu and also, Checkout Type as "Multi-product Checkout".

Please Note:- The Multi-vendor PreOrder Integration works only in the case of Multi-product Checkout and Automatic PreOrder.

DEFAULT PREORDER QUANTITY FOR PRODUCTS

As soon as you enable the "Automatic PreOrder", you will get the option to set the default preorder quantity for products that will be applied for each seller's products.

Now, once the seller products get out of stock, preorder will be enabled automatically and the quantity you configure here will be allowed for preorder for each seller's products.

RESTRICT QUANTITIES FOR PREORDER

Now, you can restrict the No. of product quantities allowed to pre-order. For this, please visit the PrOrder Controller app>>Go to Configurations>>PreOrder Configuration.

Here, you can set the product quantity limit that you want to allow for your customers to preorder.

Seller Configuration

On the seller end, the pre-order will be enabled as soon as the seller's products goes out of stock & the pre-order quantities will be default applied to products to be pre-ordered.

Seller's product that goes "out of stock", will be published in your store for preorder. Later, the seller of the product can update the preorder details while editing the product.

The seller needs to log in to the Seller Panel>>Go to Products>>Product Listing>>Click the Edit button of the product having "0" quantity>>proceed to Edit Variant...

Now, In the Variant Details section, the seller will get the option to edit variant.

Now, the seller will go to the "PreOrder Details" section and edit quantity for preorder as well as no of products accepted for preorder.

POINT TO BE NOTED

In case the seller removes the "PreOrder" option from a product in the seller panel, a preorder won't be created automatically for this seller's product.

So, in this case, the admin needs to add a preorder manually to this product.

Restrict Vendors To Create PreOrder

You can now restrict vendors from creating preorder on products in automatic preorder.

To do this, you need to visit the PreOrder Configuration menu of the app and add vendors whom you want to restrict.

To restrict multiple vendors, add comma-separated vendor names in the field.

NOTE:- It work with the automatic preorder.

Confirm the process that will stop all running and upcoming preorders of vendors.

Pre-order products:-

Sellers can now add preorder options for their products directly from their dashboard.

login to the account->products->product listing->three dots->add preorder

After clicking on "Add Preorder", you can:

a) Publish the item immediately for preorder visibility

b) Set a start and end date for the preorder window

c) Add inventory quantities specifically allocated for preorder sales

d) Add product description

Once you save the changes, a "Preorder" tag will automatically appear on the product in your product listing.

Sellers have the flexibility to edit products even after enabling preorders by

login to the account->products->product listing->three dots->edit preorder

Sellers can end a preorder campaign at any time.

login to the account->products->product listing->three dots->edit preorder->end preorder

That's all about the admin & the seller end configuration.

Customer End

To show the preorder details on your storefront, you need to add the codes to the suggested liquid files. CHECK THE COMPLETE GUIDE.

This is how the PreOrder Product appears on the product page:-

Moreover, you can create a navigation menu from your Shopify end to list all your seller's preorder products on a single page.

Once the customer order any product, he/she can view all the details in the “My Account” section.

To check their preorders, the customers need to log into their accounts and click the "PreOrder" button.

Once the customer clicks on the “Preorder” button displayed on the frontend, he/she will get the list of all the products ordered by him/her.

This includes the “Pre-order Status”, “Payment Status” and “pre-order duration”.

That's all about the Multi-vendor Marketplace PreOrder Integration.
